# Presentation Design System

## Palette
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Title: `#44546A`
- Accent: `#4472C4`
- Body: `#000000`
- Muted: `#000000`

## Fonts
- Title / headings: Calibri Light
- Body / lists: Calibri

## Type scale (pt)
- Title: 26.0
- Body: 14.0

## Layout rules
- Outer margin: 0.3 in on every edge.
- Title sits at the top; body below the title; chart below/beside the body.
- Branding logo stays in a small header/corner band, clear of text.
- Charts are placed 'contain' (preserve aspect ratio, never stretched).

## Do / Don't
- DO keep regions non-overlapping and inside the slide.
- DON'T inherit the template's content-shaped boxes — place fresh shapes.
